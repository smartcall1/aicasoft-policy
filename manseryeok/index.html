개인정보처리방침

오프라인 만세력(Offline Manseryeok, 이하 "앱")은 이름이나 연락처 같은 개인정보를 직접 수집하거나 서버에 저장하지 않아요. 앱에서 입력한 값은 기기 안에서만 처리돼요.

1. 개인정보의 처리 목적

  • 생년월일시로 만세력(명식)을 계산해 보여 주고, 저장한 명식을 다시 볼 수 있게 해요.
  • 무료로 쓸 수 있도록 광고를 보여 줘요.
  • 위 목적 밖으로는 쓰지 않아요.

2. 저장하는 값

입력한 생년월일시·이름은 이 기기에만 저장되고 서버·광고로 보내지 않아요. 지우려면 저장 목록에서 삭제하거나 앱을 삭제하면 돼요. 입력 중 초안은 이 기기에 최대 1시간 보관한 뒤 삭제해요. 회사 서버가 없어서 어디로도 보내지 않아요.

로그인이나 회원 가입은 없고, 이 앱에는 결제 기능도 없어요(광고 제거 결제는 꺼져 있어요).

3. 광고(Google AdMob)

앱은 무료 제공을 위해 Google AdMob 광고를 표시해요. 광고 제공 과정에서 Google이 광고 ID, 기기 정보, 대략적인 위치(IP 기반), 앱 상호작용, 진단 정보를 수집·이용할 수 있어요. 유럽경제지역(EEA)·영국 사용자에게는 처음 실행할 때 동의를 구하며, 설정의 "광고 개인정보 옵션"에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있어요. 기기 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 맞춤 광고를 끌 수도 있어요. 동의를 거부하거나 확인에 실패하면 앱은 광고를 요청하지 않아요. 광고는 배너와 전면 광고로 나와요.

4. 앱이 쓰는 권한

아래는 앱을 만들 때 합쳐지는 안드로이드 권한 목록(9개)이에요. 모두 설치할 때 한꺼번에 허용되는 권한이라 따로 묻는 창은 뜨지 않아요. 위치, 연락처, 사진, 마이크, 카메라 권한은 쓰지 않아요.

  • 인터넷(INTERNET): 광고를 받아 오는 데 필요해요.
  • 네트워크 상태 보기(ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): 인터넷이 연결돼 있는지 확인해서 광고를 불러올지 정해요.
  • 광고 ID 사용(AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID): 광고 제공과 측정을 위해 기기의 광고 ID를 읽어요. 휴대폰 설정에서 삭제하거나 재설정할 수 있어요.
  • 안드로이드 광고 서비스(ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): 광고 SDK가 기기의 광고 측정과 관심 주제 기능을 쓸 수 있게 하는 권한이에요.
  • 절전 방지와 백그라운드 작업(WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): 광고 SDK 같은 라이브러리가 작업을 마칠 때까지 기기가 잠들지 않게 하는 라이브러리용 권한이에요.
  • 앱 내부 보호용 권한(DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): 앱 안의 신호를 다른 앱이 엿보지 못하게 하는 권한이에요.

6. 보관 기간과 삭제

앱이 기기에 저장하는 기록은 사용자가 지우거나 앱을 삭제할 때까지 보관하며, 정해진 기간이 지나면 자동으로 지우는 기능은 없어요. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워져요. Google로 전송된 광고 데이터는 Google의 보관 정책을 따르며, 앱 삭제나 기기 설정의 광고 ID 재설정·삭제로 연결을 끊을 수 있어요. 삭제 요청은 아래 문의처로 보내 주세요.

이 앱은 안드로이드 자동 백업을 꺼 두었어요. 저장된 기록은 Google 계정 등으로 백업되지 않고, 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워져요.

7. 열람·정정·삭제 요청

  • 기기에 저장된 명식과 설정은 앱의 저장 목록에서 삭제하거나, 앱을 삭제하거나, 휴대폰 설정 > 앱 > 이 앱 > 저장공간 > 데이터 삭제로 직접 지울 수 있어요.
  • 광고 설정은 휴대폰 설정 > Google > 광고에서 바꾸거나 광고 ID를 지울 수 있어요. Google이 가진 정보는 Google 계정에서 확인하고 지울 수 있어요.
  • 열람, 정정, 삭제, 처리 정지 요청은 아래 이메일로 보내 주세요. 본인 확인 뒤 지체 없이 처리하고, 늦어도 10일 안에 처리 결과나 처리하지 못하는 사유를 알려 드려요.

Privacy Policy

Offline Manseryeok (the "App") does not directly collect personal information such as your name or contact details, and does not store it on a server. Anything you enter in the App is processed only on your device.

1. Purpose of processing

  • To calculate and show a manseryeok chart from a birth date and time, and let you view saved charts again.
  • To show ads so the App can be free.
  • We do not use information for anything beyond these purposes.

2. What the App stores

The birth date/time and name you enter are stored only on this device and are not sent to a server or to ads. To erase them, delete the entry from your saved list or uninstall the App. A draft you are typing is kept on this device for up to 1 hour and then deleted. We have no server, so none of it is sent anywhere.

There is no login or sign-up, and the App has no payment feature (the remove-ads purchase is turned off).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To stay free, the App shows Google AdMob ads. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP), app interactions, and diagnostics. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are asked for consent on first launch, and can change it any time under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. If you decline consent or consent cannot be confirmed, the App does not request ads. Ads include banner and interstitial ads.

4. Permissions the App uses

Below are the 9 Android permissions merged into the App when it is built. All are granted at install time, so no prompt appears. The App does not use location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera permissions.

  • Internet (INTERNET): needed to receive ads.
  • View network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): checks whether you are online to decide whether to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID): reads your device advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can delete or reset it in your phone settings.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): lets the ad SDK use the device ad measurement and interest topics features.
  • Keep awake and background work (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): library permissions that keep the device awake until a library such as the ad SDK finishes its work.
  • In-app protection (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): stops other apps from listening to signals inside the App.

5. Transfer of personal information abroad

For advertising, the information below is transferred abroad (to Google LLC in the USA), by purpose. To opt out, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the App.

Information transferred

Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, California 94043, USA). Google privacy inquiries: https://policies.google.com/privacy. For app-related questions, contact our privacy officer (hsindevelop@gmail.com)

Showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks. Retention follows Google's retention policy
How to refuse and the effect
You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings, and uninstalling the App stops all sending. Users in the EEA and the UK are asked for ad consent on first launch and can change it under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. If you refuse, the App still works as usual, but ads may not appear or may be non-personalized.

6. Retention and deletion

Records the App stores on your device are kept until you clear them or uninstall the App; there is no feature that deletes them automatically after a set period. They are deleted when you uninstall the App. Ad data sent to Google follows Google's retention policy; you can cut the link by uninstalling the App or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings. Send deletion requests to the contact below.

The App turns off Android automatic backup. Stored records are not backed up to your Google account or elsewhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

7. Access, correction and deletion requests

  • Saved charts and settings on your device can be erased from the saved list in the App, by uninstalling the App, or in phone Settings > Apps > this app > Storage > Clear data.
  • Ad settings can be changed, and your advertising ID deleted, in phone Settings > Google > Ads. Information Google holds can be viewed and deleted in your Google account.
  • Send requests to access, correct, delete or stop processing to the email below. After confirming it is you, we act without delay and tell you the result, or why we cannot act, within 10 days at the latest.
  • We do not hold your records, so there is almost nothing we can show or delete for you. Please clear the records on your device with the methods above.

8. Provision to third parties and outsourcing

  • We do not provide your records to third parties or outsource their processing, because your records never reach us.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): Google LLC uses ad data not only to serve and measure ads but also for its own purposes such as preventing misuse, so this is treated as provision to a third party (Google LLC), not processing we outsourced. Your advertising ID, device information and ad impression information are sent.
  • The purpose is showing ads, and retention follows Google LLC's policy.

9. Age

  • The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. It does not turn on the child-directed treatment tag in ad requests.
  • It does not ask for your age, name, or contact details, and there are no user accounts. However, the device ID and advertising ID are processed automatically to serve ads.
  • Ads are limited to the Parental Guidance (PG) rating or below, close to suitable for all ages.
  • Personalized ads depend on the ad consent process (in regions that require consent) and your Google ad settings.
  • A legal guardian of a user under 14 may request access or deletion at hsindevelop@gmail.com. If we learn that a child's information has been collected, we will delete it without delay.

10. Security measures

  • We have no server or account that receives what you enter, so we cannot see it. Your records exist only on your device.
  • The permissions the App uses are limited to what ads need.
  • Only one person, the privacy officer, handles personal information.
  • Security of ad data is managed by Google LLC under its own standards.
