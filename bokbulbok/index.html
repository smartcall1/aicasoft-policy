Privacy Policy

The First Titan (service name: AICA SOFT, the "Company") publishes this privacy policy for Quick Picks (the "App") in accordance with applicable privacy law. The App does not directly collect personal information such as your name or contact details, and does not store it on a server.

1. Purpose of processing

  • To let you use random-pick tools such as name draws, ladder, lots, team splitting, a lunch roulette and bill splitting, and to reload the name lists you saved.
  • To show ads so the App can be free.
  • Nothing is used beyond these purposes.

2. Information processed and where it is stored

  • Name groups, participant names and menu candidates you enter: stored only on your device (localStorage). The Company has no server and sends them nowhere, even if you typed another person's name.
  • Amounts and head-counts you enter on the bill-splitting screen are not saved on your device and are not sent to any server.
  • Appearance (light, dark, system), language and sound settings: stored only on your device.
  • Advertising ID, device information and ad impression information: collected by Google LLC for ads (Google AdMob). What you enter in the App is not sent.
  • Ad consent (UMP): where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, a Google consent dialog appears when you open the App, and you can change it any time under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. If you refuse or the check fails, the App does not request ads.
  • There is no login or sign-up, and the App has no payment feature. No separate analytics service (such as Firebase) is used.

3. Permissions the App uses

These are the Android permissions merged into the App. All are granted at install time, so no prompt appears. The App does not use location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera permissions.

  • Internet (INTERNET): needed to load ads.
  • View network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): checks whether you are online to decide whether to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID): reads your device advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can delete or reset it in your device settings.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): lets the ads SDK use ad measurement and interest topics.
  • Keep awake and background work (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): SDK permissions that keep the device awake until the ads SDK finishes its work.
  • App-internal protection (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): stops other apps from listening to signals inside the App.

4. Retention period

  • Records stored on your device are kept until you delete them or uninstall the App. There is no automatic deletion after a set period.
  • Ad information is kept by Google under Google's policy.
  • Android automatic backup is turned off for the App. Stored records are not backed up to your Google account or elsewhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

5. Deletion procedure and method

  • You can delete saved groups and participant names one by one in the App.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes everything stored on your device. You can also clear data under device Settings > Apps > this App > Storage. There are no separate copies.
  • Information held by Google can be viewed and deleted by the methods in "Rights of data subjects and how to exercise them".

6. Provision to third parties and outsourcing

  • The Company does not provide your records to third parties or outsource their processing, because your records never reach the Company.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): Google LLC uses ad data not only to serve and measure ads but also for its own purposes such as preventing misuse, so this is treated as provision to Google LLC, not processing outsourced by the Company. Advertising ID, device information and ad impression information are sent.
  • The purpose is serving and measuring ads, and retention follows Google LLC's policy.

8. Children

  • The App is a general app for all users and is not directed to children. The child-directed treatment tag is not turned on for ad requests.
  • The App does not ask for your age, name or contact details and does not knowingly collect information from children. Device identifiers and the advertising ID are processed automatically to serve ads.
  • Ads are limited to the Parental Guidance (PG) rating or below. Personalized ads depend on the ad consent flow (where consent is required) and your Google ads settings.
  • A legal guardian of a child can request access or deletion at hsindevelop@gmail.com. The App has no accounts or servers, so the Company holds no information itself. Ad data sent to Google follows the method in "Deletion procedure and method" above.

9. Automatically collected information (advertising ID)

  • Advertising ID: an identifier for advertising on your Android device. The ads SDK reads it using the permission (AD_ID) merged at install.
  • How to refuse: delete or reset the advertising ID under device Settings > Google > Ads.

10. Security measures

  • The Company has no server or account that receives your records, so it cannot see them. They exist only on your device.
  • The permissions the App uses are limited to what ads need.
  • Only one person, the privacy officer, handles personal information.
  • Security of ad data is managed by Google LLC under its own standards.

11. Rights of data subjects and how to exercise them

  • You can view and delete your records stored on your device in the App at any time. Uninstalling the App or using device Settings > Apps > this App > Storage > Clear data also removes them.
  • Ad personalization can be changed, or the advertising ID deleted, under device Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Where consent is required, you set ad consent in the Google dialog shown when you open the App and can change it any time under "Ad privacy options" in Settings.
  • Information held by Google can be viewed and deleted under device Settings > Google, or in your Google account.
  • To request access, correction, deletion or a halt to processing, email hsindevelop@gmail.com. We act without delay after confirming it is you, and tell you the result or the reason we cannot act within 10 days at the latest.
  • We hold none of your records, so there is very little we can view or delete for you. Please delete records on your device by the methods above.

以下は本アプリに含まれるAndroidの権限です。すべてインストール時にまとめて許可されるため、確認画面は表示されません。位置情報・連絡先・写真・マイク・カメラの権限は使いません。

  • 広告IDの利用（AD_ID、ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID）：広告の配信と測定のため、端末の広告IDを読み取ります。端末の設定で削除・リセットできます。

  • スリープ防止とバックグラウンド処理（WAKE_LOCK、FOREGROUND_SERVICE）：広告SDKが処理を終えるまで端末をスリープさせないSDK用の権限です。
  • アプリ内部の保護用権限（DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION）：アプリ内の信号を他のアプリが盗み見できないようにします。

4. 保有期間

  • 本アプリはAndroidの自動バックアップを使わない設定です。保存された記録はGoogleアカウント等にバックアップされず、アプリを削除すると一緒に消えます。

  • 앱이 쓰는 권한은 광고에 필요한 것으로 줄였습니다.
  • 개인정보를 다루는 사람은 개인정보 보호책임자 한 명으로 한정합니다.
  • 광고 데이터의 보안은 Google LLC가 자체 기준으로 관리합니다.

11. 정보주체의 권리와 행사 방법

  • 사용자는 언제든 이 기기에 저장된 자기 기록을 앱에서 보고 지울 수 있습니다. 앱을 삭제하거나 휴대폰 설정 > 앱 > 이 앱 > 저장공간 > 데이터 삭제로도 지울 수 있습니다.
  • 광고 개인 설정은 휴대폰 설정 > Google > 광고에서 바꾸거나 광고 ID를 지울 수 있습니다.
  • 동의가 필요한 지역에서는 앱을 열 때 뜨는 Google 동의 창에서 광고 동의를 정하고, 설정의 '광고 개인정보 옵션'에서 언제든 다시 바꿀 수 있습니다.
  • Google이 가진 정보는 휴대폰 설정 > Google, 또는 Google 계정에서 확인하고 지울 수 있습니다.
  • 열람, 정정, 삭제, 처리 정지 요청은 이메일(hsindevelop@gmail.com)로 보내 주십시오. 요청하신 분이 본인인지 확인한 뒤 지체 없이 처리하고, 늦어도 10일 안에 처리 결과나 처리하지 못하는 사유를 알려 드립니다.
  • 회사는 사용자의 기록을 갖고 있지 않아서 회사가 열람하거나 지워 드릴 수 있는 정보가 거의 없습니다. 기기 안의 기록은 위 방법으로 직접 지워 주십시오.
