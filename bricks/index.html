개인정보처리방침

벽돌깨기(이하 "앱")는 이름이나 연락처 같은 개인정보를 직접 수집하거나 서버에 저장하지 않아요. 앱에서 입력한 값은 기기 안에서만 처리돼요.

1. 개인정보의 처리 목적

  • 벽돌깨기 게임을 하고, 깬 스테이지를 이어서 할 수 있게 보여 줘요.
  • 무료로 쓸 수 있도록 광고를 보여 줘요.
  • 광고 제거를 구매했는지 확인해요.
  • 위 목적 밖으로는 쓰지 않아요.

2. 저장하는 값

깬 스테이지, 누적 클리어·플레이 횟수, 광고 제거 구매 여부(확인한 시각 포함), 화면 모드·소리·진동·언어 설정은 이 기기(localStorage)에만 저장해요. 회사 서버가 없어서 어디로도 보내지 않아요.

로그인이나 회원 가입은 없어요. 광고 제거는 한 번만 결제하는 상품이고, 결제는 Google Play가 처리해서 카드 정보 같은 결제 수단 정보는 앱과 회사에 전달되지 않아요. 환불은 Google Play 환불 정책을 따릅니다.

3. 광고(Google AdMob)

앱은 무료 제공을 위해 Google AdMob 광고(배너, 전면, 보상형)를 표시해요. 플레이 중에는 광고가 나오지 않고, 보상형 광고는 직접 눌렀을 때만 나와요. 광고 제거를 구매하면 배너와 전면 광고는 더 나오지 않아요. 광고 제공 과정에서 Google이 광고 ID, 기기 정보, 대략적인 위치(IP 기반), 앱 상호작용, 진단 정보를 수집·이용할 수 있어요. 유럽경제지역(EEA)·영국 사용자에게는 처음 실행할 때 동의를 구하며, 설정의 "광고 개인정보 옵션"에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있어요. 기기 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 맞춤 광고를 끌 수도 있어요. 동의를 거부하거나 확인에 실패하면 앱은 광고를 요청하지 않아요.

4. 앱이 쓰는 권한

앱이 직접 선언한 권한은 인터넷(INTERNET) 하나예요. 광고 SDK와 결제 라이브러리가 앱을 만들 때 권한을 더할 수 있고, 아래는 그 종류예요. 모두 설치할 때 한꺼번에 허용되는 권한이라 따로 묻는 창은 뜨지 않아요. 위치, 연락처, 사진, 마이크, 카메라 권한은 쓰지 않아요.

  • 인터넷(INTERNET): 광고를 받아 오고 결제 상태를 확인하는 데 필요해요.
  • 광고 SDK가 더하는 권한(네트워크 상태 보기, 광고 ID 사용 등): 인터넷 연결을 확인하고, 광고 제공과 측정을 위해 기기의 광고 ID를 읽어요. 광고 ID는 휴대폰 설정에서 삭제하거나 재설정할 수 있어요.
  • 결제 라이브러리가 더하는 권한(Google Play 결제): 광고 제거를 Google Play로 결제하고 구매 여부를 확인하는 데 필요해요.

6. 보관 기간과 삭제

앱이 기기에 저장하는 기록은 사용자가 지우거나 앱을 삭제할 때까지 보관하며, 정해진 기간이 지나면 자동으로 지우는 기능은 없어요. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워져요. Google로 전송된 광고 데이터는 Google의 보관 정책을 따르며, 앱 삭제나 기기 설정의 광고 ID 재설정·삭제로 연결을 끊을 수 있어요. 삭제 요청은 아래 문의처로 보내 주세요.

이 앱은 안드로이드 자동 백업을 꺼 두었어요. 저장된 기록은 Google 계정 등으로 백업되지 않고, 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워져요.

7. 열람·정정·삭제 요청

  • 기기에 저장된 기록은 앱을 삭제하거나 휴대폰 설정 > 앱 > 이 앱 > 저장공간 > 데이터 삭제로 직접 지울 수 있어요. 앱 안에 기록을 지우는 화면은 따로 없어요.
  • 광고 설정은 휴대폰 설정 > Google > 광고에서 바꾸거나 광고 ID를 지울 수 있어요. 광고 제거 구매 내역은 Google Play에서 확인할 수 있어요. Google이 가진 정보는 Google 계정에서 확인하고 지울 수 있어요.
  • 열람, 정정, 삭제, 처리 정지 요청은 아래 이메일로 보내 주세요. 본인 확인 뒤 지체 없이 처리하고, 늦어도 10일 안에 처리 결과나 처리하지 못하는 사유를 알려 드려요.
  • 회사는 사용자의 기록을 갖고 있지 않아서 회사가 열람하거나 지워 드릴 수 있는 정보가 거의 없어요. 기기 안의 기록은 위 방법으로 직접 지워 주세요.

Privacy Policy

Brick Breaker (the "App") does not directly collect personal information such as your name or contact details, and does not store it on a server. Anything you enter in the App is processed only on your device.

1. Purpose of processing

  • To let you play the brick-breaker game and pick up from the stages you have cleared.
  • To show ads so the App can be free.
  • To check whether you have bought Remove Ads.
  • We do not use information for anything beyond these purposes.

2. What the App stores

Stages cleared, the number of clears and games played, whether you have bought Remove Ads (and when it was last checked), and your display-mode, sound, vibration and language settings are stored only on this device (localStorage). We have no server, so none of it is sent anywhere.

There is no login or sign-up. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play, so payment details such as card information never reach the App or us. Refunds follow the Google Play refund policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To stay free, the App shows Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded). No ads appear while you are playing, and rewarded ads appear only when you tap to watch one. If you buy Remove Ads, banner and interstitial ads stop. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP), app interactions, and diagnostics. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are asked for consent on first launch, and can change it any time under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. If you decline consent or consent cannot be confirmed, the App does not request ads.

4. Permissions the App uses

The only permission the App declares itself is Internet (INTERNET). The ad SDK and the billing library can add permissions when the App is built, and the kinds are listed below. All are granted at install time, so no prompt appears. The App does not use location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera permissions.

  • Internet (INTERNET): needed to receive ads and to check purchase status.
  • Permissions added by the ad SDK (view network state, advertising ID, etc.): checks that you are online, and reads your device advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can delete or reset the advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • Permission added by the billing library (Google Play billing): needed to pay for Remove Ads through Google Play and to check whether you own it.

5. Transfer of personal information abroad

For advertising, the information below is transferred abroad (to Google LLC in the USA), by purpose. To opt out, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the App.

Users in the EEA and the UK are asked for ad consent on first launch and can change it under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. If you refuse, the App still works as usual, but ads may not appear or may be non-personalized.

6. Retention and deletion

Records the App stores on your device are kept until you clear them or uninstall the App; there is no feature that deletes them automatically after a set period. They are deleted when you uninstall the App. Ad data sent to Google follows Google's retention policy; you can cut the link by uninstalling the App or by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings. Send deletion requests to the contact below.

The App turns off Android automatic backup. Stored records are not backed up to your Google account or elsewhere, and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

7. Access, correction and deletion requests

  • Records on your device can be erased by uninstalling the App, or in phone Settings > Apps > this app > Storage > Clear data. The App has no screen for clearing records.
  • Ad settings can be changed, and your advertising ID deleted, in phone Settings > Google > Ads. Your Remove Ads purchase can be viewed in Google Play. Information Google holds can be viewed and deleted in your Google account.
  • Send requests to access, correct, delete or stop processing to the email below. After confirming it is you, we act without delay and tell you the result, or why we cannot act, within 10 days at the latest.
  • We do not hold your records, so there is almost nothing we can show or delete for you. Please clear the records on your device with the methods above.

8. Provision to third parties and outsourcing

  • We do not provide your records to third parties or outsource their processing, because your records never reach us.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): Google LLC uses ad data not only to serve and measure ads but also for its own purposes such as preventing misuse, so this is treated as provision to a third party (Google LLC), not processing we outsourced. Your advertising ID, device information and ad impression information are sent.
  • The purpose is showing ads, and retention follows Google LLC's policy.
  • Payment (Google Play): Google Play processes the Remove Ads payment. The App and we do not receive payment details; the App only asks Google Play whether you own it and stores that result on your device.

9. Age

  • The App is a general app for all users, not an app made for children. It does not turn on the child-directed treatment tag in ad requests.
  • It does not ask your age and does not separately collect personal information from children under 14.
  • Ads are limited to the Parental Guidance (PG) rating or below, close to suitable for all ages.
  • Personalized ads depend on the ad consent process (in regions that require consent) and your Google ad settings.
  • If you learn that information from a child under 14 has been collected, please tell the privacy officer below. If we hold the information you identify, we delete it without delay.

10. Security measures

  • We have no server or account that receives what you enter, so we cannot see it. Your records stay only on your device.
  • The permissions the App uses are limited to what advertising and payment need.
  • Only one person, the privacy officer, handles personal information.
  • Security of ad data is managed by Google LLC under its own standards.
