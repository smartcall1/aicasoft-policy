Bingo Caller Voice Privacy Policy

Effective date 2026-10-12

Bingo Caller Voice ("the app") has no server and no account system. The developer does not collect or store personal information such as your name or contact details. Your game state (bingo settings, called numbers, and call history) is stored only on your device (local storage) and is never sent to us. Google processes the advertising ID, approximate location, and other data described below to serve ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free, supported by banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP address), app interactions, and diagnostic data. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are asked for consent on first launch and can change it anytime under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. If consent is declined or cannot be confirmed, the app does not request ads.

Voice

The app calls out bingo numbers by playing pre-recorded voice clips that are stored in the app. The clips are played inside the app and nothing is sent outside the app for this. Only if a clip is missing does the app use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone; depending on that engine and its settings (for example, Google Text-to-Speech), some voices may then be generated over the network by the engine's provider under its own settings and privacy policy, not by the app, and the app does not send the spoken text to us. The app does not use the microphone and does not record any audio.

International data transfers

For advertising, the information below is sent to Google LLC in the United States. To opt out, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the app.

How to opt out, and what happens
You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings, and uninstalling the app stops all transfers. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are asked for ad consent on first launch and can change it under 'Ad privacy options' in Settings. If you opt out, the app still works the same; you may see no ads or non-personalized ads.

Retention and deletion

Game state and settings the app stores on your device are deleted when you uninstall the app. Advertising data sent to Google is kept under Google's retention policies. Once the app is uninstalled, nothing more is sent (you can also cut the link for ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in device settings). To request deletion of data already sent, contact us using the details below.

Age

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask your age, and the app collects no name or contact information. Device and advertising IDs are processed automatically to show ads.
