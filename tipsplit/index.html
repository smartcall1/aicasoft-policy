FairTip Privacy Policy

Effective date 2026-10-17

FairTip ("the app") does not collect personal information such as your name or contact details, and does not store it on a server. What you enter in the app is processed only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free, supported by ads from Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP address), app interactions, and diagnostic data. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are asked for consent on first launch and can change it anytime under "Ad privacy options" in Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. If consent is declined or cannot be confirmed, the app does not request ads.

International data transfers

For advertising, the information below is sent to Google LLC in the United States. To opt out, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or uninstall the app.

How to opt out, and what happens
You can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings, and uninstalling the app stops all transfers. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are asked for ad consent on first launch and can change it under 'Ad privacy options' in Settings. If you opt out, the app still works the same; you may see no ads or non-personalized ads.

Permissions the app uses

These are the Android permissions merged into the app (checked in the merged manifest of the build). All are granted at install time, so no prompt appears. The app does not use location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, storage, or notification permissions.

  • Internet (INTERNET): needed to load ads.
  • View network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): checks whether you are online to decide whether to load ads.
  • Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK): lets the ads SDK finish its work while the device is active.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): declared by a library the app includes (the ads SDK); the app itself does not run a background service.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID): reads your device advertising ID to serve and measure ads. You can delete or reset it in your device settings.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): lets the ads SDK use ad measurement and interest topics.
  • App-internal protection (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION): stops other apps from listening to signals inside the app. This is declared by the app itself.

Backup

The app turns off Android backup (allowBackup is false in the merged manifest). Settings stored on your device are not copied to Google cloud backup or to a new phone through device transfer, and you start fresh after reinstalling or moving to a new phone.

Retention and deletion

Settings the app stores on your device are deleted when you uninstall the app. Advertising data sent to Google is kept under Google's retention policies. Once the app is uninstalled, nothing more is sent (you can also cut the link for ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in device settings). To request deletion of data already sent, contact us using the details below.
